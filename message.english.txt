TMIDI Messages - SSP Plugin

Detects MIDI playback from TMIDI Player via DDE and sends song
titles to ghost characters through Sakura Script.

Usage: Just install and load in SSP. When TMIDI Player plays a
MIDI file, the ghost will speak the song title.

You can customize the message template in sstp_sample.txt.

Requirements: TMIDI Player, SSP with a ghost loaded.

Compile: cl /LD /O2 /MD /Fe:tmidi_msg.dll tmidi_msg.c user32.lib
