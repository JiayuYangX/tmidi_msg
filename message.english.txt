TMIDI Messages - SSP Plugin

Detects playback from TMIDI Player via DDE and sends track
information to ghost characters through Sakura Script.

Usage: Install and use.
When TMIDI Player starts playback, the ghost will announce
the track.

You can customize the message template in sstp_sample.txt.

Requirements: TMIDI Player, SSP with a ghost loaded.

Compile: cl /LD /O2 /MD /Fe:tmidi_msg.dll tmidi_msg.c user32.lib
